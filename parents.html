Parent Portal

See the Whole Picture

One login gives you a live view of every child you have enrolled, so there is no waiting until the end of term to find out how things are going.

  • Continuous assessment scores and term results
  • Daily attendance and punctuality record
  • Fee statements, receipts and outstanding balances
  • Teacher comments and behaviour notes
  • Meeting bookings and school announcements

Access is limited to parents and guardians of enrolled students.

Getting Your Portal Account

Your login is issued by the school office once your child's place is confirmed, using the email address you registered with.

There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by the school office for parents and guardians of enrolled students only, so nobody outside the school can register.

Working Together

How We Keep You Informed

Parent-Teacher Meetings

Twice a term, after each formal assessment. Book a slot with each subject teacher through the portal.

SMS & Email Alerts

Same-day notice of absence, early closure or any incident involving your child. Update your number in the portal.

Class WhatsApp Groups

Each form teacher runs a group for day-to-day notices. Ask the school office to be added.

Termly Newsletter

A round-up of results, events, trips and staff news, emailed in the last week of every term.

Open Days

Two per session, when you can sit in on lessons and see your child’s work in their own classroom.

Direct Line

The school office answers from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM on weekdays. No phone tree, a person picks up.

Fees & Payment

Fees are due within the first two weeks of each term.

  • Pay by bank transfer, POS at the bursary, or online through the portal
  • Instalment plans are available; arrange them with the bursar before the term starts
  • 10% reduction on tuition for a third and any subsequent child
  • Receipts appear in the portal within 24 hours of payment clearing

Questions

Frequently Asked by Parents

What do I do if my child will be absent?

Call or message the school office before 8:00 AM on the day. For absences longer than two days, please send a written note or a medical report when your child returns.

How do I raise a concern about my child’s progress?

Start with the form teacher, who sees your child every day. If the matter is unresolved after a week, contact the Vice Principal (Academics) through the school office.

Is there a school bus on my route?

We run routes across Lagos Mainland and Island. Call the school office with your address and the transport officer will confirm the nearest pick-up point and the termly fee.

What is the policy on phones?

Phones are not permitted during lesson hours. Boarding students hand phones to their house parent and have supervised access at set times each evening.

Who do I speak to about fees?

The bursary, on +234 801 234 5679 or bursary@brightfuture.edu.ng. Please arrange any instalment plan before the term begins.
