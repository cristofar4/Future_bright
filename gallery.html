Share Your Photos

Were You There?

Parents, students and alumni are welcome to send in photographs from school events. Send them to the school office and, with your permission, we will add the best of them here and to the termly newsletter.

  • Email images to media@brightfuture.edu.ng
  • Tell us the event, the date and who is in the photo
  • We only publish photographs of students with consent on file

Contact the Media Team

Photography & Consent

Every family is asked about photography consent at enrolment, and the answer can be changed at any time by writing to the school office.

Where consent has not been given, that student does not appear in any published photograph — on this website, in the newsletter, or on social media.
