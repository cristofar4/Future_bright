What’s Inside

Your Results, Your Resources

The student portal is where your school record lives. It is updated within 48 hours of every assessment.

  • Continuous assessment scores and end-of-term results
  • Your class timetable and examination schedule
  • Lesson notes, past questions and assignment uploads
  • Attendance record and house points
  • Library catalogue and book reservations

Forgotten your password? Ask your form teacher or email ict@brightfuture.edu.ng.

e-Learning

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Virtual Classroom

Live lessons and recordings for revision weeks, mid-term breaks and any day the school is closed.

Past Questions

WAEC, NECO and JAMB papers from the last ten years, with worked solutions for Mathematics and the sciences.

CBT Practice

Timed computer-based tests that mirror the real JAMB interface, available in the ICT lab and from home.

House System

Four Houses, One School

Every student belongs to a house from their first day. Houses compete in sports, debate, quiz and cultural day, and they look after each other in between.

Unity House

Blue • “Together we rise”

Integrity House

Beyond Lessons

Clubs & Societies

Coding & Robotics

Python, web basics and robotics kits. Meets Wednesdays, 3:30 PM in the ICT lab.

Debate & Public Speaking

Prepares teams for inter-school competitions. Meets Tuesdays, 3:30 PM.

Science Club

Experiments, science fair projects and the annual STEM exhibition. Meets Thursdays.

Sports Teams

Football, basketball, athletics and table tennis. Training four afternoons a week.

Press & Literary Club

Produces the termly school magazine and runs the creative writing prize.

Community Service

Monthly outreach projects with neighbouring primary schools and the local health centre.

Code of Conduct

What we expect of every student, every day.

  • Arrive on time, in full uniform, ready to learn
  • Treat every person on campus with respect
  • Do your own work, honestly
  • Look after school property and the environment
  • Report bullying or unsafe behaviour to any staff member

Worried about something? Speak to your form teacher, the school counsellor, or any member of staff you trust.
