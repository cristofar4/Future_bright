About Our School

Since 2015 Bright Future Secondary School has grown from a single block of classrooms into a community of more than 500 students, held together by one belief: every child can do more than they think.

Who We Are

Nurturing Minds,
Shaping Futures

Bright Future Secondary School is a co-educational day and boarding school on Education Road, Lagos. We follow the Nigerian national curriculum, enriched with ICT, entrepreneurship and leadership training, and we prepare students for the WAEC, NECO and JAMB examinations.

Classes are capped at 25 students so that every child is known personally. Teachers track progress week by week, and parents hear from us long before a small difficulty becomes a real one.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality secondary education that develops the mind and the character of every student, and equips them to contribute to their community.

Our Vision

To be the secondary school of choice in Lagos, known for academic strength, integrity and graduates who lead with confidence and compassion.

Our Values

Excellence in every effort. Integrity when no one is watching. Respect across differences. Service to others. Resilience when things are hard.

Our Story

A Decade of Growth

2015: The first intake

The school opened with 48 students, six teachers and one block of classrooms on Education Road.

2018: First WAEC cohort

Our first set of final-year students sat WAEC, with 94% earning five credits including English and Mathematics.

2021: Science and ICT block

Purpose-built physics, chemistry and biology laboratories opened alongside a 40-seat computer laboratory.

2025: 500 students and counting

Ten years on, the school serves more than 500 students across JSS1 to SS3, with alumni in universities at home and abroad.

Campus

Facilities That Support Learning

Science Laboratories

Separate physics, chemistry and biology labs, each equipped for practical work from JSS3 upward.

ICT Laboratory

Forty networked workstations with broadband, used for coding, digital literacy and CBT practice.

Library

Over 6,000 titles, a quiet study area and a reading programme that runs through every year group.

Sports Field

A full-size football pitch, basketball court and athletics track used for PE and inter-house competition.

School Transport

A managed bus service covering the main routes across Lagos, with tracked vehicles and trained drivers.

Safety & Care

Gated campus, CCTV coverage, a resident school nurse and a trained safeguarding lead on duty daily.
