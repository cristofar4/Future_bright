How to Apply

Four Steps to a Place

Submit the application form

Complete the form below or collect a paper copy from the school office. A non-refundable application fee of ₦10,000 applies.

Sit the entrance assessment

Candidates take a written assessment in English Language and Mathematics, plus Basic Science for SS1 applicants. Assessments run on the first Saturday of each month at 9:00 AM.

Meet us for an interview

A short, friendly conversation with the student and at least one parent or guardian. This usually happens the same week as the assessment.

Accept your offer

Successful candidates receive an offer letter within seven working days. Your place is confirmed once the acceptance fee is paid and documents are verified.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or sworn declaration of age
  • Last two terms’ result sheets from the current school
  • Transfer certificate (for students joining JSS2-SS2)
  • Four recent passport photographs
  • Completed medical and immunisation record
  • Parent/guardian identification

Questions

Admissions FAQ

Can my child join mid-session?

Yes. Transfers into JSS2-SS2 are accepted at the start of any term where a place is available. Your child will sit the same entrance assessment and will need a transfer certificate from their previous school.

What is the entrance assessment like?

A written paper in English Language and Mathematics lasting about two hours, with an additional Basic Science section for SS1 applicants. It covers the curriculum your child has already been taught, so no special preparation is needed.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes, for students from JSS2 upward. Boarding houses are supervised by resident house parents, with prep sessions every evening and a resident nurse on campus.

Is there a sibling discount?

Families with three or more children enrolled at the same time receive a 10% reduction on tuition for the third and any subsequent child.

Can we visit before applying?

Please do. Campus tours run every Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00 AM during term time. Call the admissions office or use the contact form to book.
