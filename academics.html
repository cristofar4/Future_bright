Academics

A well-rounded curriculum built to develop knowledge, skills and character, and to prepare every student for WAEC, NECO, JAMB and whatever comes after.

Our Academics

Quality Education for a Brighter Tomorrow

We follow the Nigerian national curriculum across two stages. Junior Secondary (JSS1-JSS3) gives every student a broad foundation and ends with the Basic Education Certificate Examination. Senior Secondary (SS1-SS3) lets students specialise in Science, Commercial or Arts while keeping the core subjects everyone needs.

Lessons are 40 minutes, classes are capped at 25, and every student sits a formal assessment twice a term so that nobody drifts.

Programmes

Two Stages, One Standard

Junior Secondary (JSS1 - JSS3)

A broad foundation for every student, leading to the BECE.

  • English Language, Mathematics and Basic Science
  • Basic Technology, Business Studies and Agricultural Science
  • Social Studies, Civic Education and Nigerian History
  • Computer Studies, French and Creative Arts
  • Physical & Health Education and Religious Studies

Senior Secondary (SS1 - SS3)

Core subjects plus a chosen track, leading to WAEC, NECO and JAMB.

  • Core for all: English, Mathematics, Civic Education
  • Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Mathematics
  • Commercial: Accounting, Commerce, Economics
  • Arts: Literature, Government, History, CRS/IRS
  • Data Processing and Entrepreneurship across all tracks

How We Teach

Our Approach in the Classroom

Small Classes

A cap of 25 students per class means teachers can work with individuals, not just the room.

Practical First

Science is taught with the equipment in students' hands from JSS3 onward, not just described on a board.

Speak and Defend

Debate, presentation and structured discussion are built into every subject, not left to the debate club.

Digital Fluency

Every student uses the ICT lab weekly, including CBT practice ahead of JAMB.

Continuous Assessment

Two formal assessments each term, plus weekly class work, feed a running record parents can see.

Extra Support

After-school clinics in Mathematics and English run four days a week, free to any student who needs them.
