Finding the School

We are on Education Road, a short drive from the main expressway. Visitor parking is available inside the gate. Please bring photo identification and sign in at the security post.

  • Campus tours every Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00 AM in term time
  • Open days twice a session, with dates published in the newsletter
  • Visitors are asked to arrive ten minutes early to sign in
